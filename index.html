About me

Hi, I'm Padmaja. I dissect what makes a brand successful.

I am currently a digital marketing student with a passion for dissecting what makes a brand successful. I believe the best way to master the craft is to put theory into practice, which is why I created this blog.

Here, I publish regular brand teardowns—deep dives and case studies that analyze the strategies behind the marketing campaigns we see every day.

I am always learning and constantly looking for new perspectives. Whether you are a fellow student, a marketing professional, or just curious about the industry or you are a brand/business, I’m glad you’re here. Let’s explore the world of marketing together.
